# Immediate insight should optimize for changed decisions, not accumulated information

## Observation
Short-horizon research is most useful when it answers what changed now, not when it merely adds more documents.

Useful outputs include:
- what changed
- what remains uncertain
- what was surprising
- what became possible
- what became obsolete
- what is the cheapest useful test

## Interpretation
A research loop should be evaluated by whether it changes a decision, priority, hypothesis, or test plan.

## Decision relevance
Design monitoring and research agents to emit decision deltas rather than generic summaries.

## Limits
This does not replace deep historical or medium-term analysis. Short, medium, and long time horizons should coexist.
