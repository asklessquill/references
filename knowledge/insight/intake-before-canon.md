# New evidence should enter through intake before canon

## Observation

Fresh benchmarks, external reports, experiments, and observations arrive faster than they can be classified or validated. Writing them directly into canonical guidance turns recency into authority.

## Interpretation

A pre-canonical intake layer preserves speed without sacrificing governance.

## Decision relevance

Capture provenance, date, task context, model or system version if known, confidence, and triage status. Later promote, reject, supersede, or retain as raw evidence without rewriting its origin.

## Limits

An intake area needs periodic triage or it becomes an unsearchable backlog.
