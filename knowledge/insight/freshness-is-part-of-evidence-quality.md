# Freshness is part of evidence quality

## Observation

Evidence can be correct historically and still be poor support for a current decision in fast-changing domains such as AI models, pricing, limits, products, and operational APIs.

## Interpretation

Evidence quality includes temporal relevance, not only source credibility.

## Decision relevance

Record observed time, effective time when known, and expected decay rate. Route volatile claims through short-horizon refresh paths while preserving medium- and long-horizon context separately.

## Limits

Newer evidence is not automatically better; recency must be balanced against reliability and sample quality.
