# Faster information requires stronger noise controls

## Observation

Short-horizon sources provide earlier signals but also contain more duplication, speculation, transient errors, and context-poor claims than slower curated research.

## Interpretation

An immediate insight loop should optimize decision-changing evidence, not raw update volume.

## Decision relevance

Preserve provenance, distinguish firsthand from reposted claims, cluster duplicates, mark uncertainty, compare against prior state, and escalate only changes likely to affect a decision.

## Limits

Early weak signals can still be valuable; noise control should preserve uncertainty rather than filtering everything unconfirmed.
