# A useful update should explain what changed for the decision

## Observation

Large summaries often preserve information while failing to change action. A compact decision-oriented update can be more useful when it answers a small set of questions.

## Interpretation

For fast-moving topics, a good change packet asks:

- What changed?
- What remains uncertain?
- What was surprising?
- What became possible?
- What became obsolete?
- What is the cheapest useful test now?

## Decision relevance

Use this structure when the reader must decide what to do next rather than merely stay informed.

## Limits

Deep research may require broader evidence and historical context; this packet is a decision interface, not a substitute for the evidence base.
