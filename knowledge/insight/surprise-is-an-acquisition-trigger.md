# Surprise is a signal to acquire more evidence

## Observation

An observation that strongly conflicts with the system's current model can carry more learning value than another confirming datapoint.

## Interpretation

Surprise should not immediately rewrite canon, but it should increase attention and trigger targeted acquisition or a cheap discriminating experiment.

## Decision relevance

Record what expectation was violated, possible explanations, decision impact, and the cheapest evidence that could separate them.

## Limits

Noise, bad data, and measurement defects also create surprise; escalation should preserve uncertainty.
