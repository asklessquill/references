# Evidence is valuable when it changes what you would do

## Observation
Evidence volume is a weak proxy for research value. A small observation that changes a route, model choice, boundary, or next experiment can be more useful than a large report that changes nothing.

## Interpretation
Research systems should preserve the link between evidence and the decision it could affect.

## Decision relevance
For each meaningful finding, capture:
- prior assumption
- new evidence
- changed or unchanged decision
- confidence
- next cheapest validation step

## Limits
Decision impact does not make evidence true. Provenance and uncertainty must still be preserved.
