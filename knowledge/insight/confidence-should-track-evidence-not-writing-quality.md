# Confidence should track evidence, not how convincing the prose sounds

## Observation

Strong models can produce polished explanations for weakly supported interpretations. Writing quality can therefore create false confidence.

## Interpretation

Confidence metadata should be anchored to provenance, independent confirmation, reproducibility, and applicability rather than rhetorical coherence.

## Decision relevance

Separate observed fact, inference, and hypothesis. Raise confidence when evidence improves, not merely when a stronger model rewrites the entry.

## Limits

Confidence remains a judgment and should be calibrated over time against outcomes.
