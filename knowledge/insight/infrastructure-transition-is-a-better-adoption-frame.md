# Treat transformative AI adoption as an infrastructure transition, not a tool campaign

## Observation

Technologies such as the internet became unavoidable not because organizations optimized usage percentages forever, but because workflows, expectations, and competitive baselines changed around them.

## Interpretation

For general-purpose AI, the deeper question is which work becomes structurally different and when non-adoption creates a capability gap.

## Decision relevance

Study replaced workflows, new defaults, dependency formation, and tipping points rather than only adoption counts. Use the transition itself as the unit of analysis.

## Limits

Historical analogies can mislead; AI has different economics, risks, regulation, and organizational effects.
