# A general system can form capabilities at the point of need

## Observation

Pre-building every capability that might someday be useful is expensive and encourages speculative architecture. Entering a real problem first reveals which capability is actually missing.

## Interpretation

A system that can discover, borrow, build, or compose capabilities on demand may be more general than a system with a large fixed catalog.

## Decision relevance

Evaluate generality by how quickly and safely the system can become what a new situation requires, not only by the number of capabilities already installed.

## Limits

On-demand formation depends on strong discovery, authority, validation, and recovery; some foundational capabilities still need to exist in advance.
