# Research, build, and operate are distinct responsibilities even when they form one loop

## Observation

Interpreting the world, constructing a solution, and activating it in human or operational context require different evidence and failure handling.

## Interpretation

Treating them as one generic agent role hides where uncertainty, implementation risk, and operational coordination actually live.

## Decision relevance

Keep responsibilities distinct while making handoffs cheap: research produces interpreted evidence, build produces verified capability, operations turns available capability into authorized real-world use and feeds outcomes back.

## Limits

One actor may perform multiple responsibilities in small systems; semantic separation still helps evaluation and later decomposition.
