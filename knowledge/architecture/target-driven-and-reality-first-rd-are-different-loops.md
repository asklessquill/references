# Target-driven R&D and reality-first R&D are different loops

## Observation

Two useful development modes can look similar while optimizing for different outcomes:

- target-driven: choose a concrete outcome, then build the capabilities needed to achieve it economically
- reality-first: enter the world early, encounter unmet needs, then become what is required in response

## Interpretation

Mixing these loops can make success criteria unstable. One optimizes toward a known target; the other optimizes learning from contact with reality.

## Decision relevance

Run them as distinct workstreams with separate metrics and let evidence flow between them rather than forcing one planning model onto both.

## Limits

The loops can converge later and share infrastructure; separation is about purpose and evaluation, not permanent organizational isolation.
