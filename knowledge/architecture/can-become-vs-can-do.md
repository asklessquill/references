# Design for what the system can become, not only what it can do now

## Observation
Feature-oriented architectures optimize a fixed list of capabilities. Adaptive systems may instead need to discover, acquire, compose, and retire capabilities as real needs emerge.

## Interpretation
The more durable architectural question can be: can the system become the kind of system required by the next real problem?

## Decision relevance
Favor mechanisms for:
- capability discovery
- safe composition
- replaceable implementations
- evidence-based acquisition
- retirement of obsolete capability
- learning from outcomes

## Limits
Infinite extensibility is not a practical requirement. Boundaries, safety, cost, and authority remain necessary.
