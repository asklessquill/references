# AI should do decomposition without forcing the human to live in the decomposition

## Observation

Humans often communicate with compact concepts whose internal structure is rich. AI can decompose those concepts for execution, but returning every internal substep to the human can increase cognitive cost.

## Interpretation

A productive interface preserves the human's chosen abstraction level while allowing the AI to reason at finer granularity internally.

## Decision relevance

Report at the level needed for the next human decision. Expose lower-level detail on demand, for evidence, or when ambiguity matters.

## Limits

High-risk decisions may require more explicit decomposition even when it increases cognitive load.
