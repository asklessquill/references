# The value of multi-model access is optionality, not merely variety

## Observation

Different models can be stronger at coding, long-context analysis, tool use, style, speed, cost, or skeptical review. A multi-model surface exposes those differences to routing.

## Interpretation

Model diversity is useful when outcomes are measured and the selector can learn which differences matter. Without routing evidence, more models can become choice overhead.

## Decision relevance

Treat multi-model access as an exploration asset. Capture comparative episodes and retain the ability to specialize routes rather than searching for one permanent winner.

## Limits

Operational simplicity may outweigh optionality when one model consistently dominates the relevant workload.
