# Stored experience should change future routing, not merely decorate history

## Observation

A system may accumulate successful routes, failures, and lessons without using them when choosing the next path.

## Interpretation

Experience becomes operational knowledge only when it changes selection, uncertainty, evidence expectations, or failure avoidance.

## Decision relevance

Test the same request with and without prior experience and verify that relevant history can alter route choice or confidence for an explainable reason.

## Limits

Past success can become stale. Experience must retain provenance and context so it is not generalized blindly.
