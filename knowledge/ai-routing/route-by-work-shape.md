# Choose an AI surface by the shape of the work

## Observation

Modern AI products expose several surfaces that may share models but differ in interaction pattern: conversational co-reasoning, long-running general work, and repository-centered software engineering.

## Interpretation

The surface is part of the routing decision. A strong model in the wrong work surface can create unnecessary friction.

## Decision relevance

Use conversational surfaces for joint framing and decisions, general work agents for multi-step jobs that should reach a finished artifact, and coding agents for repository inspection, commands, tests, and implementation.

## Limits

Boundaries overlap and product capabilities evolve. Route by the dominant work shape rather than rigid product labels.
