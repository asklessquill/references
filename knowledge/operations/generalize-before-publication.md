# Generalize reusable lessons before publishing private experience

## Observation

Private projects can generate broadly useful architecture and operational knowledge while containing names, internal state, credentials, business details, or unreleased implementation evidence that should not be public.

## Interpretation

The reusable asset is often the principle, failure mode, or decision pattern rather than the private event itself.

## Decision relevance

Before public publication, remove identifying or sensitive details, avoid private repository internals, preserve the epistemic status, and rewrite the lesson so it stands on its own.

## Limits

Generalization can remove evidence needed to judge validity. Keep richer provenance privately when necessary and do not overstate the public abstraction.
