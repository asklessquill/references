# Account replacement can be used as a recovery test instead of a migration

## Observation

Moving to a fresh AI account is risky if important project meaning still lives only in the old account's chats or memory. Keeping the old account intact while testing a fresh one avoids destructive migration.

## Interpretation

Treat the new account as a replacement-brain test: can it reconstruct the current world from durable evidence without inherited conversational state?

## Decision relevance

Do not merge or delete the old environment first. Prove reconstruction, compare interpretations, repair missing canonical artifacts, and only then decide whether to migrate active work.

## Limits

Account-level product features and policies vary by vendor and must be checked separately.
