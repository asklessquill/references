# Remove human relay work without removing human authority

## Observation
Many automation systems reduce manual copying and triggering but also accidentally erase meaningful human decisions.

## Interpretation
A useful target is not Human Involvement = 0. It is Human Relay Count -> 0 while Human Decisions > 0 where authority matters.

## Decision relevance
Automate transport, repetition, state handoff, and routine observation. Preserve explicit human authority for purpose, boundaries, irreversible actions, risk acceptance, and major direction changes.

## Limits
Low-risk systems may require little human authority. High-risk domains may require more than this minimal pattern.
