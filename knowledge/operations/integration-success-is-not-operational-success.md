# Successful integration is not successful operation

## Observation

Authentication, API calls, connector search, and data retrieval prove that an integration path exists. They do not prove the system can use that capability to achieve the intended operational outcome.

## Interpretation

Integration is infrastructure evidence; operational success is outcome evidence.

## Decision relevance

After proving connectivity, test a realistic decision or workflow under actual authority boundaries and observe whether the intended outcome occurs with acceptable cost and recovery behavior.

## Limits

Connectivity tests remain valuable prerequisites and should not be skipped; they simply prove a narrower claim.
