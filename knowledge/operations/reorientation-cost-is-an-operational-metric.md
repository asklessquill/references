# The cost of figuring out where work stands is an operational metric

## Observation

A project can be technically productive while repeatedly forcing humans and fresh agents to reconstruct current state from commits, chats, branches, and partial reports.

## Interpretation

Start-of-work reorientation is real operational waste. Reducing it increases usable development time without increasing model capacity.

## Decision relevance

Measure how long a competent fresh reader needs to answer current state, trusted evidence, blockers, and next authorized action. Improve projections and closeout until that time becomes small.

## Limits

Very small projects may not justify dedicated current-state artifacts.
