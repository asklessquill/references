# Make an experiment real enough to learn, not complete enough to impress

## Observation

Planning and abstraction can continue indefinitely without exposing the assumptions that only appear in contact with a real runtime, user, service, cost, or failure.

## Interpretation

The first experiment should maximize decision-relevant learning per unit of irreversible effort.

## Decision relevance

Choose the cheapest action that creates genuine evidence about the uncertain part of the system. Preserve the observation and let it change the next design.

## Limits

Minimum reality is not permission to ignore safety, authorization, or containment. Experiments should be real within bounded consequences.
