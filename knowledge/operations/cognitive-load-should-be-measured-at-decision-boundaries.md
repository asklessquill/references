# Measure cognitive load at decision boundaries, not by prompt count

## Observation

AI can increase total interaction while decreasing the amount of complexity a human must actively hold to make the next decision.

## Interpretation

A useful cognitive-load metric asks how much reconstruction, detail management, and context switching the human must perform before a consequential choice.

## Decision relevance

Measure reorientation time, number of sources the human must manually reconcile, clarification loops, unresolved ambiguities, and whether reporting stays at the human's chosen abstraction level.

## Limits

Cognitive load is partly subjective and task-dependent; combine qualitative reports with repeatable operational proxies.
