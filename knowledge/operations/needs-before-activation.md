# Activation should begin from the real need, not from available capability

## Observation
Operational systems often start with what tools can do and then search for places to use them. This can create activity without useful real-world change.

## Interpretation
A lightweight needs assessment should precede activation: what outcome is needed, by whom, under what constraints, with what authority, and what would count as a meaningful change.

## Decision relevance
Before automating an action, establish:
- actual need
- target outcome
- authority boundary
- timing and cost constraints
- observable success condition
- smallest useful intervention

## Limits
For exploratory experiments the need may intentionally be weak or provisional. The requirement is clarity about why the action is being taken.
