# Annual discounts can hide the cost of lost optionality

## Observation

A lower monthly equivalent can still be more expensive strategically when the platform, model landscape, or system architecture is changing quickly.

## Interpretation

The real comparison is not monthly price versus discounted monthly price. It also includes the value of being able to switch vendors, plans, models, or architecture after new evidence appears.

## Decision relevance

Treat annual commitment as purchasing capacity while selling future optionality. The faster the surrounding technology changes, the more valuable that optionality may be.

## Limits

Stable workloads with proven platform fit may justify long commitments and discounts.
