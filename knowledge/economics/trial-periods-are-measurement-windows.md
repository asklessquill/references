# Trial periods should be treated as measurement windows

## Observation

A free or discounted access period can easily become simple consumption without producing evidence for the eventual purchase decision.

## Interpretation

The scarce asset in a trial is not only free capacity but the chance to establish a baseline under known constraints.

## Decision relevance

Define what the trial should measure: accepted output per cycle, model dependence, quota pressure, rework, human time, portability, or platform-specific value. Preserve results so the paid decision compares evidence rather than impressions.

## Limits

Trials may have unusual limits or promotional behavior that differs from steady-state paid service.
