# An AI subscription is a capacity contract, not just feature access

## Observation

Two plans may expose similar models while differing materially in rate limits, agent capacity, context, workspace capabilities, billing commitment, and operational continuity.

## Interpretation

A subscription should be evaluated as a contract for usable capacity under real workflow patterns, not as a feature checklist.

## Decision relevance

Compare:

- sustained and burst capacity
- reset cadence
- interruption cost
- access to specialist models
- context limits
- tool and agent surfaces
- commitment period
- portability and lock-in

## Limits

Vendor limits and pricing change frequently; current documentation must be rechecked before purchase.
