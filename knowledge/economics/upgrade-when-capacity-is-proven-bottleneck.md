# Upgrade capacity when capacity is the proven bottleneck

## Observation

Higher limits are valuable only when the system can convert additional capacity into useful progress. During unstable architecture phases, more throughput can accelerate rework or contamination.

## Interpretation

Capacity upgrades should follow evidence that quality, direction, and recovery are sufficiently stable and that interruption or shortage is now the limiting factor.

## Decision relevance

Before upgrading, ask:

- Is useful work blocked by capacity rather than uncertainty?
- Is additional throughput likely to produce accepted artifacts?
- Are recovery and canonicalization strong enough to absorb faster output?

## Limits

Short-term strategic boosts can still be rational when a time-sensitive checkpoint must be reached.
