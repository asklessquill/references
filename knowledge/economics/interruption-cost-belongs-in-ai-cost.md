# AI cost includes interruption cost, not only subscription price

## Observation

Usage limits can interrupt a task after expensive context loading, local setup, implementation, or review. The monetary price of the plan may be smaller than the human and system cost of losing continuity.

## Interpretation

Effective AI cost includes waiting, reorientation, duplicated setup, abandoned partial work, and delayed decisions.

## Decision relevance

When comparing plans or routing models, include expected interruption frequency and recovery cost. A higher-priced plan can be cheaper if it reliably avoids high-cost breaks; a lower-priced plan can be better when pauses are harmless or useful.

## Limits

Interruption cost is workflow-specific and should be measured rather than assumed.
