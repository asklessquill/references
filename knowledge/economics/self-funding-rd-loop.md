# A self-improving system eventually needs an economic feedback loop

## Observation
Technical autonomy can keep consuming model, infrastructure, and human resources without proving that the resulting capability creates enough real-world value to sustain itself.

## Interpretation
A meaningful maturity threshold is reached when the value created by the system can fund a material portion of its own research and operating cost.

## Decision relevance
Track separately:
- model and infrastructure cost
- human intervention cost
- measurable external value created
- reinvestment into capability improvement
- time to recover experimentation cost

## Limits
Not every research system should optimize for profit. Public-interest, scientific, learning, and safety goals may use different value functions.
