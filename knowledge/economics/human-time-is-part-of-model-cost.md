# Human time is part of AI model cost

## Observation

A cheaper model or plan can require more prompt repair, monitoring, context reloading, review, and recovery. A more expensive route can sometimes reduce those costs.

## Interpretation

Model economics should include human attention as a scarce resource, not treat it as free overhead.

## Decision relevance

When comparing routes, capture human intervention count, active minutes, rework, and cognitive reorientation alongside direct subscription or credit cost.

## Limits

Human time can be difficult to estimate precisely; rough consistent measurement is often more useful than false precision.
